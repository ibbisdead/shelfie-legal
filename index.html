Shelfie: Sort & Match

A bright, satisfying sorting puzzle for Android.

Drag goods between shelves and line up three of a kind to clear them, before the timer runs out. Plan around back rows, moving shelves, shutters, curtains, padlocks and tricky Super Hard levels, with Meemo cheering you on.
